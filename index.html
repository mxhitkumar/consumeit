Why ConsumeIT

Reasons that hold up after the contract is signed

No “world-class” claims. These are the specific things that change how your project runs.

Technology and marketing under one roof

The team that builds the site is the team that ranks and advertises it. No hand-offs between a developer who won’t touch analytics and an agency that can’t touch code.

Performance is a requirement, not an upsell

Core Web Vitals, image weight, script budget and mobile behaviour are part of the build spec — because a slow page loses both rankings and buyers.

Decisions come from data

Search Console, analytics and ad platform data drive what we change next. Every engagement ships with reporting you can read without us in the room.

Automation capability in-house

We can wire a chatbot to your enquiry inbox or push form leads into your CRM — the same team, on the same project, without a second vendor.

Direct, jargon-free communication

You talk to the people doing the work. Scope, timeline and what’s blocked are stated plainly, in writing.

Support after launch

Maintenance windows, security updates and performance checks are part of every website package — a launch is a starting point, not a handover.

How we work

A process you can hold us to

Five stages, each with something you can see or read at the end of it.

01

Discover

We map the business goal, the audience and what currently gets in the way — then agree what success looks like in numbers before any design starts.

02

Plan

Scope, sitemap, timeline and budget in writing. You know what ships, when it ships, and what is explicitly out of scope.

03

Build

Design and development in reviewable stages. You see working pages on a staging link, not a slide deck, and feedback goes in as we go.

04

Launch

Analytics, search console, tracking events, schema and performance checks are verified before go-live — not bolted on afterwards.

05

Grow

SEO, campaigns and automation run against the numbers agreed in step one, with monthly reporting and a clear next-action list.

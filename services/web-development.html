    01 In short

    Your website is where someone decides whether to get in touch. We make it clear what you do and easy to act on, especially on a phone.

    02 What this helps with

    Sound familiar?

    1. 01No website, or an old oneWe build a clean site, or redesign the one you have.
    2. 02People visit but do not callWe make it clear what to do next, with forms and buttons that are easy to find.
    3. 03Hard to use on a phoneWe build pages that are easy to read and tap on small screens.
    4. 04You need a page for one offerWe make a focused page or form for one campaign or one offer.

    03 What you get

    Websites and pages made to be used

    Here is what we can build and set up for you.

    Websites

    Pages that explain what you do.

    • Business websites
    • Landing pages (one page for one offer)
    • Sites for schools and coaching institutes
    • Clinic and healthcare sites
    • Redesigns of existing sites

    04 How we do it

    Talk, plan, make, check.

    1. 1

      Talk

      Share who you want to reach and what you want visitors to do.

    2. 2

      Plan

      We agree which pages you need and what each one should say.

    3. 3

      Make

      We design and build the site, and make it work well on phones.

    4. 4

      Check

      We check everything works. Then we agree what to look at next.

    05 Good to know

    Straight answers, no surprises.

    • We agree the plan first

      Before work starts, we agree with you what we will do, when, and how we will report back.

    • No promises about enquiries

      A clear site makes it easy to get in touch. We cannot promise a number of enquiries, because that depends on many things.

    • Clinic sites stay careful

      For clinic and healthcare sites, we keep the words careful and avoid medical claims.

    • Paid tools are quoted first

      If your work needs a paid tool, we will say so in the quote before you decide.

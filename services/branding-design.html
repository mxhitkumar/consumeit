    04 How we do it

    Talk, plan, make, check.

    1. 1

      Talk

      Share what you do, who you want to reach and what you already have.

    2. 2

      Plan

      We agree what to make, how often and how it should look and sound.

    3. 3

      Make

      We edit, design and write, following the plan we agreed.

    4. 4

      Check

      We look at what is working and agree what to do next.

    05 Good to know

    Straight answers, no surprises.

    • We agree the plan first

      Before work starts, we agree with you what we will do, when, and how we will report back.

    • No promises about views or followers

      Nobody can honestly promise views, followers or sales. We make clear, steady content and look at what works.

    • AI speeds things up, people check

      AI can make work faster. People stay in charge of the ideas and the quality.

    • Paid tools are quoted first

      If your work needs a paid tool, we will say so in the quote before you decide.

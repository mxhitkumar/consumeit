Reach is easy to buy and easy to report. We run paid and social as a demand system — the right audience, an ad that earns the click, and a landing page that does not waste it — measured end to end on cost per lead.

The problem

Where marketing budget usually leaks

  • Ads point at a homepage instead of a page built for the promise the ad made, so the click is paid for and then thrown away.
  • Conversion tracking is missing or broken, so the platform optimises towards clicks rather than enquiries — and the reporting cannot be trusted either way.
  • Audiences and keywords are set once and never revisited, so spend drifts towards whatever is cheapest rather than whatever converts.
  • Social posting is consistent but strategically empty: no offer, no funnel, no path from follower to enquiry.
  • Creative fatigue is ignored, so the same ad runs until performance collapses.

Our approach

Fix the destination first, then buy the traffic

Before spending your budget we check where the clicks land — page speed, message match, and whether conversion tracking actually fires. Then campaigns are built around a specific action, with audiences and keywords researched rather than guessed, creative refreshed on a schedule, and budget moved towards what produces enquiries. Because we build landing pages too, fixing the destination is part of the same engagement.

Process

How the engagement runs

The first weeks are about instrumentation and structure. After that it is a monthly cycle of testing and reallocation.

01

Audit & tracking setup

We review existing accounts, landing pages and tracking. Conversion events are fixed or built before any budget is committed — spending without measurement is how budgets disappear.

02

Strategy & structure

Objectives, audiences, offers and campaign structure agreed in writing, with a target cost per lead to judge against.

03

Creative & landing pages

Ad creative and copy produced, and the destination page checked or rebuilt so the promise in the ad matches what loads.

04

Launch & optimise

Campaigns go live in controlled stages. Budget shifts towards what converts; underperforming audiences, keywords and creative are cut.

05

Report & scale

Monthly reporting on spend, cost per lead and return, with a clear recommendation on where the next rupee should go.

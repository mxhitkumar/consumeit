    05 Good to know

    Straight answers, no surprises.

    • Ad spend is separate from our fee

      Ad spend is the money that goes to the ad platform. It is separate from what we charge to plan and manage your ads.

    • No promises about followers or enquiries

      We cannot promise followers, views, enquiries or sales. We plan carefully, test and look at the numbers.

    • We agree the plan first

      Before work starts, we agree with you what we will do, when, and how we will report back.

    • Paid tools are quoted first

      If your work needs a paid tool, we will say so in the quote before you decide.

The problem

Why SEO retainers so often disappoint

  • The reported keywords are the easy ones — brand terms and long strings nobody searches — while the terms your buyers use go unmentioned.
  • Technical problems are never fixed because the SEO vendor cannot touch the website and the developer has other priorities.
  • Content is published to a schedule rather than to demand, so volume goes up and rankings do not.
  • Page speed and Core Web Vitals are ignored, even though they affect both crawling and the conversion rate of the traffic you do win.
  • Nobody separates what SEO delivered from what the ads delivered, so the retainer can never be judged.

Our approach

Fix the foundation, then earn the positions

We start with a technical audit and actually implement the fixes — we are the development team too, so nothing waits in someone else's backlog. Then keyword and intent mapping against genuine demand, on-page optimisation, internal linking, schema, and a content plan built around what your buyers search before they buy. Reporting separates organic performance from paid so you can see what the retainer is doing.

Capabilities

What the work covers

Technical, on-page, local and content SEO, run as one programme rather than separate line items.

Technical SEO audit & fixes

Crawlability, indexation, site architecture, canonicals, redirects, duplicate content, sitemaps, robots directives and Core Web Vitals — diagnosed and then fixed.

Keyword & intent mapping

Monthly reporting on rankings, impressions, clicks and organic conversions, with a prioritised list of what changes next and why.

Process

How the engagement runs

SEO is a compounding programme. The first month is heavier on diagnosis; the rest is execution and iteration.

01

Audit

A full technical and content audit — crawl data, Search Console history, index coverage, Core Web Vitals, competitor positioning. You get findings ranked by impact against effort, not a 90-page PDF.

02

Research & mapping

Keyword and intent research mapped to existing and planned pages, so every target term has one page that owns it and no two pages compete.

03

Technical remediation

We implement the fixes — structure, speed, schema, redirects, indexation. Because we build websites too, this does not sit in a queue.

04

On-page & content

Existing pages optimised, new pages written against mapped intent, internal linking built so authority reaches the pages that need it.

05

Measure & iterate

Monthly review of rankings, organic traffic and conversions, with the next month's priorities agreed from the data rather than a fixed template.

Expected outcomes

What you should see

  • A technically clean site: indexable, crawlable, fast, with no duplicate or conflicting signals.
  • Growth in impressions and clicks for terms with buying intent, not just brand searches.
  • Local visibility in maps and “near me” searches, where that applies to your business.
  • Rich results where the page type supports them — FAQs, breadcrumbs, products, reviews.
  • Monthly reporting you can read yourself, separating organic from paid performance.
  • Full ownership of Search Console, Analytics and the Business Profile.

Timelines: technical fixes can show movement within weeks; competitive rankings generally take three to six months and longer in crowded markets. We will not promise first-page positions in thirty days, because doing so honestly is not possible.

    We tidy up your website, find the words people search and check how your pages do.

    01 In short

    When someone types a question into Google, your pages should be easy to find and easy to understand. We tidy the basics, find the words people use and watch how your pages do.

    02 What this helps with

    Sound familiar?

    1. 01People search but find othersWe look at what people type and what your site says.
    2. 02Hidden website problemsWe check the basics that help search engines read your pages.
    3. 03Unsure which words matterWe research what people search for and match it to your pages.
    4. 04No view of how you are doingWe watch how your pages show up in search and explain it simply.

    03 What you get

    Everything we cover for Google search

    Here is what is included in this work.

    Google search help

    SEO means helping search engines understand your pages.

    • Website basics, so Google can find and read your pages
    • The title, short description and headings of each page
    • Search research: the words people type to find you
    • How your pages are organised and linked together
    • Watching your search results over time
    • Planning for people searching from other countries

    04 How we do it

    Talk, plan, make, check.

    1. 1

      Talk

      Tell us what you do and who should find you. We look at your site as it is today.

    2. 2

      Plan

      We research what people search for and agree the pages and words to work on.

    3. 3

      Make

      We fix the basics and tidy the page titles, descriptions and headings.

    4. 4

      Check

      We watch how your pages do and agree what to change.

    05 Good to know

    Straight answers, no surprises.

    • No promises about rankings

      Nobody controls Google. We cannot promise first-page rankings or a number of visitors.

    • It takes time

      Search results change slowly and for many reasons. We agree what to look at and when we report before work starts.

    • AI speeds things up, people check

      AI can make work faster. People stay in charge of the ideas and the quality.

    • Paid tools are quoted first

      If your work needs a paid tool, we will say so in the quote before you decide.

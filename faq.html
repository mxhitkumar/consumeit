Frequently asked questions

Section 01

General

Automate: AI chatbots, workflow automation and system integrations. Branding, graphic design and video editing support all three. The full list is on the services page.

We are based in Rohtak, Haryana, India, and work with businesses and content creators across India and internationally. Engagements run remotely over calls, shared documents and staging links, so location is rarely a constraint.

Section 02

Websites & development

A template-based starter site usually ships in one to two weeks. A custom static or SEO-ready site is typically two to four weeks. Web applications, e-commerce and Android apps are scoped individually — you get a dated timeline before work starts. The largest variable is how quickly content and feedback come back.

Every site we build is designed mobile-first and tested from 320px upward, because most traffic in India arrives on a phone. We also build Android applications with API integration and Play Store release support.

Performance is part of the build spec, not an add-on. We budget script and image weight, set explicit image dimensions to avoid layout shift, lazy-load below-the-fold media and check Core Web Vitals before launch — because page speed affects both rankings and conversion.

That depends on the build, and we agree it up front. Every project includes access handover, a walkthrough and written documentation, and every website package includes a maintenance window during which we handle updates for you.

Section 03

SEO & marketing

SEO makes a site easier for search engines to understand and easier for people to choose. Technical work fixes crawling, indexing, structure and speed. On-page work aligns pages with what your buyers actually search for. Content and internal linking build authority on those topics. The result is traffic with buying intent that does not stop when an ad budget does.

Technical fixes can show movement in weeks. Competitive rankings and sustained organic growth generally take three to six months, sometimes longer in crowded markets. Anyone promising first-page rankings in thirty days is either targeting terms nobody searches or is not being straight with you.

We can run campaigns from ₹100 per day in ad spend, which is paid directly to Google or Meta and is always separate from our management fee. Realistically, more spend gives the platforms more data to optimise against, so we will advise a sensible floor for your market rather than let a budget run too thin to learn from.

Yes, always. Accounts are created under your ownership wherever possible, and you keep full access to analytics, Search Console and ad platforms during and after the engagement. Your data does not leave with the agency.

Monthly, against what was agreed at the start — reach, engagement, follower growth and, where the funnel supports it, enquiries attributed to social. You also see the content calendar in advance rather than after the fact.

    02 Ways to work

    Three ways to work with us

    Not sure which one fits? Tell us what you need and we will suggest one.

    1. 01One projectOne thing done, such as a set of videos, a batch of designs, a website or a campaign. We quote for that and nothing else.
    2. 02A monthly planSteady work, such as regular posts, videos, designs or ad management. We agree what is included each month, then quote it.
    3. 03The Content Creation ProgramA live program on Google Meet for people who want to start creating content themselves. $149. Dates and what is included are confirmed before you pay.→

    03 Shown separately

    Two costs we keep apart

    We list these on their own, so you always know what you are paying for.

    Ad spend

    The money you spend on ads goes straight to the ad platform, such as Facebook, Instagram or Google. Our fee for planning and managing the ads is separate.

    Tools and subscriptions

    If your work needs a paid tool, we will say so in the quote before you decide.

    Both are written in your quote.

    04 Before you pay

    What you get before you pay

    You should know what you are buying. So before any money changes hands, you have these four things.

    1. 01A written plan. What we will do, and why.
    2. 02What you will receive. Listed clearly, so nothing is a guess.
    3. 03When it will be ready. We agree the timing with you before work starts.
    4. 04How we report back. How and when you hear from us.

    Scope, timing and reporting are agreed with you before work starts.

Pricing FAQs

How pricing actually works here

Because an honest number depends on scope. A five-page site and a fifteen-page site with custom SEO content are both “a website”. Rather than publish a figure we would immediately have to qualify, we publish exactly what each package contains and quote in writing once we know what you actually need — usually within one business day.

No, and no agency should tell you otherwise. Management fees cover strategy, creative, setup, optimisation and reporting. Ad spend goes directly from you to Google or Meta, out of your own account, so every rupee is visible to you. Campaigns can run from ₹100 per day, though we will advise a realistic floor for your market.

Yes. Most engagements end up as a combination — a website build plus a monthly SEO retainer, or social management plus video editing. Tell us the outcome you need and we will build the scope around it rather than pushing you into the nearest listed tier.

Project work is split across milestones — a starting instalment, then payments as agreed stages are delivered. Retainers are billed monthly. Everything is stated in the written scope before work begins.

SEO and social media need time to compound, so we recommend a minimum of three months to judge them fairly — but the specific terms are agreed in your scope, not imposed by default.

Ask anyway. Small pieces of work — a landing page, a performance fix, a set of thumbnails — are quoted individually. There is no minimum engagement size.

    What the Business Owner Starter Plan is

    A digital foundation package for businesses that are starting from nothing online — or from a profile picture and a phone number.

    It exists because the alternative is expensive in a way that is hard to see: a freelancer for the logo, someone else for the website, a cousin for the Instagram page, and three months of your own time spent coordinating them into something that still does not look like one company.

    Here, one team produces every asset against one identity, in one month, and hands it over with a strategy for what to do next.

    Who it’s for

    Built for businesses at the starting line

    • Entrepreneurs and startups launching for the first time
    • Small businesses with no digital presence beyond a phone number
    • Established offline businesses moving online properly
    • Anyone who would rather not coordinate multiple vendors
    • Businesses that need to look credible before their first big pitch
